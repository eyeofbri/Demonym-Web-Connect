    ABOUT // BRIAN + DEMONYM

    Something I wanted to exist, so I started building it.

    I'm Brian. I spend a lot of my normal work life building websites, WordPress tools, interactive pieces, and the sort of odd little technical solutions that sit somewhere between design and development. Demonym is one of the projects where I get to take all of that curiosity and point it at something completely for fun.

    It started with the Cardputer ADV and the idea of making a small creature game that actually feels at home on the device instead of feeling like a tiny port of something else. Since then it has grown into habitats, training, roaming, battles, device-to-device connections, generated creatures, and now this web connection layer. I really enjoy the point where a project stops being an experiment and starts feeling like its own little world.

    Demonym Connect is an extension of that same idea. I wanted the Cardputer to be able to reach outside itself without losing what makes the hardware version fun. The goal isn't to replace the device with a browser. It's to make the browser another place the device can connect to: battle somebody, eventually find ranked games, and give the project room to grow.

    I love making things, especially projects where design, code, games, weird hardware, and a little bit of unnecessary experimentation can all overlap. The Cardputer ADV has been a great excuse to keep doing exactly that.
